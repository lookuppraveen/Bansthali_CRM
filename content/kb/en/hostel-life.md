# Hostel life at Banasthali

Banasthali Vidyapith is fully residential — every student lives on campus. Hostels here are called **bhawans** (Sanskrit for "abode"), each named after a river, a saint or a national figure.

## Hostels (bhawans)

Some of the main student bhawans:

- **Chandra Bhawan** — for first-year undergraduate students, twin-sharing rooms
- **Sharda Bhawan** — senior undergraduate students
- **Vidya Bhawan** — postgraduate students
- **Meera Bhawan** — B.Ed and Diploma students
- **Ganga / Yamuna / Kaveri Bhawans** — programme-mixed bhawans for accommodating growth cycles

Each bhawan has a **Warden** (senior staff), **Assistant Warden**, and student **prefects**. Room allocation is done based on programme, year, and stated preference on the hostel form.

## Facilities

- **Mess** — vegetarian, three meals per day, weekly menu published on the notice board and portal
- **Common room** — TV, indoor games, newspapers, magazines
- **Study room** — 24×7 quiet study space
- **Wi-Fi** — campus-wide Wi-Fi with a per-student daily quota
- **Laundry** — self-laundry area on each floor; paid laundry service also available
- **Medical care** — 24×7 nurse on duty; visiting doctor daily; ambulance for emergencies
- **Security** — gated campus with 24×7 wardens and campus security

## Timings and rules

- **Room-in time** — 7:30 PM (winter), 8:00 PM (summer)
- **Study hours** — 7:00 – 9:30 PM daily
- **Lights-out** — 10:30 PM in first-year bhawans; senior bhawans have flexibility
- **Sunday and holidays** — outings allowed with warden approval; day-scholar visits by prior notice

## Visitors and travel

- **Parent visits** — permitted on weekends and holidays; check into the guest house in advance
- **Guest house** — on-campus accommodation for parents, bookable through the portal
- **Home travel** — students travel home for semester breaks and holidays; the University assists with group travel booking during peak dates

## What to bring / not to bring

**Recommended:**
- Bedding (mattress is provided, cover it with your own sheets)
- Personal toiletries and towels
- Study materials, laptop
- Traditional attire (kurta / salwar-kameez is standard)
- One good sports outfit

**Not permitted:**
- Alcohol, tobacco, drugs (zero-tolerance)
- Electric heaters, induction stoves, gas cylinders
- Loud speakers, DJ equipment
- Non-vegetarian food in the mess area

## Panchmukhi (five-fold) daily routine

Banasthali's five-fold education (Panchmukhi Shiksha) is embedded in the hostel routine — physical (yoga / horse-riding / sports), aesthetic (music / arts), moral (prayer meetings), practical (crafts / community service), and intellectual (academics). Every student participates in these activities as part of daily hostel life.
