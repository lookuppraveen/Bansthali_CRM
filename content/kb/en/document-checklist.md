# Document checklist for verification

After counselling and provisional seat allocation, applicants must complete **document verification** on campus (or online, depending on the programme). Original documents are checked against the copies uploaded during application.

## Documents required (all applicants)

1. **10+2 Marksheet and Certificate** — original + one photocopy
2. **10th Standard Marksheet and Certificate** — original + one photocopy
3. **BUAT Admit Card** (if BUAT was appeared) — printout
4. **BUAT Score Card** — printout from your portal
5. **Character Certificate** — from the head of your last institution, on their letterhead, issued within the last 6 months
6. **Transfer Certificate (TC)** — from your last institution
7. **Migration Certificate** — if coming from a board other than the one your last institution used
8. **Aadhaar card** — original + photocopy
9. **Passport-size photographs** — 8 copies (recent, coloured, white background)
10. **Admission letter** — printout from the portal

## Additional (if applicable)

- **Category certificate** — SC / ST / OBC / EWS as per Government of India norms; latest date
- **Income certificate** — for fee waivers and government scholarships
- **Domicile / residence certificate** — for state-quota seats
- **Sports / cultural achievement certificates** — for extracurricular consideration and clubs
- **Medical fitness certificate** — from a registered medical practitioner, dated within 30 days
- **Guardian's consent letter** — if the guardian is not the biological parent

## For international / NRI students

- **Passport** — original + photocopy of first, last, and stamped pages
- **Student visa** — original + photocopy
- **Equivalence certificate** — from AIU (Association of Indian Universities) if your 10+2 is from a foreign board
- **PIO / OCI card** — if applicable

## Format of documents

- Originals must be presented in person for physical verification.
- Photocopies should be **self-attested** (signed with today's date).
- Certificates should be on the issuer's letterhead, signed and stamped.
- If a certificate is in a regional language, provide a notarised English translation.

## Verification queue

If any document is pending or has an issue, our verification team will raise a query on your portal:

- **Pending** — you need to upload / bring the document
- **Query raised** — the document has an issue (mismatch, expired, illegible); please address and re-submit
- **Verified** — accepted, no further action needed

You can see the live status of each document in the "Documents" section of your student portal.

## After verification

Once **all** documents are Verified and your fee is paid, your admission is confirmed. Your student ID is issued by the University ERP within 24 hours and appears on your portal.
