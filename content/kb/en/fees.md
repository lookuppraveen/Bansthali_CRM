# Fees at Banasthali Vidyapith

Banasthali is a fully residential university, so the annual fee bundles tuition, hostel, mess and basic amenities. Exact numbers vary by programme and cycle; the figures below are indicative for the 2026–27 admission cycle.

## Indicative annual fee (2026–27)

| Programme | Approx. annual fee (₹) |
|---|---|
| Vidyapith (school level, VI–XII) | 1,10,000 – 1,45,000 |
| B.A. / B.Sc. / B.Com | 1,40,000 – 1,60,000 |
| BBA / BCA | 1,55,000 – 1,80,000 |
| B.Tech (Computer Science / IT / EE / Bioinformatics) | 2,10,000 – 2,60,000 |
| B.Ed | 1,45,000 – 1,65,000 |
| LL.B / LL.M | 1,60,000 – 1,85,000 |
| M.Sc / M.A. | 1,50,000 – 1,80,000 |
| MBA | 2,50,000 – 3,00,000 |
| Ph.D | 1,00,000 – 1,20,000 |

## What's included

- **Tuition fee**
- **Hostel room** (twin-sharing standard; single-sharing available on request at additional cost)
- **Mess** (vegetarian; three meals per day)
- **Library and lab access**
- **Basic medical care through the campus health centre**

## What's NOT included

- Textbooks and stationery
- Travel to and from campus
- Laundry (self / paid service available)
- Optional extra-curricular / competition activities
- Programme-specific supplies (e.g. lab consumables beyond basic, art materials)

## Payment schedule

- **Admission fee** — payable within 7 days of receiving the offer letter to confirm your seat.
- **Semester fee** — due on 5 September (Sem 1) and 5 February (Sem 2). Late payment invites a fine.
- **Hostel deposit** — one-time refundable deposit at first admission.

## Payment methods

Fees are paid through the University's payment gateway integrated with the admission and student portal. Accepted:

- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Net banking (all major banks)
- Debit / credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Rupay)
- Bank transfer (NEFT / RTGS) for annual lump-sum

Once a payment succeeds, the status reflects in your portal within 5 minutes and in the ERP within 24 hours.

## Scholarships and fee waivers

Banasthali offers merit and need-based scholarships:

- **Merit scholarships** — for BUAT top-rankers and academic toppers each year
- **Need-based fee waivers** — through the student welfare office; apply with income proof
- **State and central scholarship schemes** — SC/ST/OBC/EWS as per Government of India norms

Apply for scholarships during or shortly after admission; the deadline is usually 30 September.
