# Campus safety at Banasthali Vidyapith

Banasthali is a women's-only, fully residential campus. Safety and wellbeing of every student is the University's first priority, and the environment is designed around this from the ground up.

## What makes the campus safe

- **Fully gated campus** — only students, staff and vetted visitors can enter; guests need prior authorisation
- **24×7 wardens** in every hostel; every bhawan has a resident warden on the same floor as students
- **Campus security** — trained security staff at gates and patrolling the campus round the clock
- **CCTV coverage** on gates, corridors, common areas, sports facilities and academic blocks
- **Women's-only campus** — no male students; male staff and workmen only in designated academic and service areas
- **Emergency medical care** — 24×7 nurse on duty, visiting doctors, ambulance and tie-ups with hospitals in Jaipur

## Wellbeing support

- **Student counselling centre** — trained counsellors for academic stress, homesickness, personal issues, all conversations confidential
- **Peer mentoring** — senior students mentor first-year students in each bhawan
- **Health centre** — routine health check-ups, vaccinations, gynaecological care, mental-health screenings
- **Nutrition** — dietician-planned mess menu; special diets on medical advice

## For parents

Parents are first-class stakeholders here. The University maintains regular communication with parents through:

- The parent portal — consent-bound view of your daughter's admission and onboarding
- Warden calls for any medical or wellbeing concern
- Newsletters and updates from the Admissions and Dean of Students offices
- Open visits on weekends and holidays (please book the guest house in advance)

## Rules that support safety

- No going out of campus after 6:00 PM without warden permission
- Group outings preferred over solo outings
- Register overnight leave with the warden and confirm with parents
- Zero-tolerance for substance abuse and any form of harassment; grievance redressal mechanism as per UGC guidelines

## Grievance redressal

Any student can raise a concern — academic, personal, harassment or otherwise — through:

- Direct approach to the warden or Dean of Students
- The University's confidential grievance portal
- The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) for issues under the Sexual Harassment (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013
