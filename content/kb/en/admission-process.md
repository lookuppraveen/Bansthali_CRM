# Admission Process at Banasthali Vidyapith

Banasthali Vidyapith is a fully residential women's university in Rajasthan. The admission cycle runs from January to August each year and follows a structured funnel: enquiry → application → Banasthali University Aptitude Test (BUAT) → merit list → counselling → document verification → enrolment.

## Stages of the admission funnel

1. **Enquiry** — Register your interest through the University website, education fair, phone, WhatsApp, referral, or by walking in to the admissions office.
2. **Nurturing** — Our counsellors help you evaluate programme fit, share brochures and answer follow-up questions.
3. **Application** — Submit the online application form on banasthali.org with fee payment. Both online and offline applications are supported.
4. **BUAT** — Appear for the Banasthali University Aptitude Test at your chosen centre. Not all programmes require BUAT — check the programme-specific requirements.
5. **Merit list** — A merit list is published programme-wise based on BUAT score and normalised 10+2 aggregate. Publication happens 7–10 days after the BUAT result.
6. **Counselling** — Called candidates attend a counselling session where they confirm programme preference and receive a seat allocation.
7. **Verification** — Original documents are verified against uploaded copies.
8. **Enrolment** — Pay the admission fee to confirm your seat. A student ID is issued through the University ERP.

## Programmes offered

Banasthali has an unusually wide academic spread — school-level Vidyapith education, diplomas, undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD across:

- **Arts & Social Sciences** — BA, MA in English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Political Science, Economics, Sociology, Psychology, History
- **Science** — B.Sc, M.Sc, PhD in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Statistics, Botany, Zoology
- **Life Sciences** — B.Sc, M.Sc, PhD in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Bioinformatics
- **Mathematics & Computing / Applied Computer Technology** — BCA, MCA, M.Sc IT
- **Technology (Engineering)** — B.Tech in Computer Science, IT, Electronics, Bioinformatics
- **Management & Commerce** — BBA, MBA, B.Com, M.Com
- **Education** — B.Ed, M.Ed, D.El.Ed
- **Law** — LL.B, LL.M
- **Music & Performing Arts** — B.A. Music, M.A. Music (Vocal, Instrumental, Dance)
- **Fine Arts** — B.Des, M.Des
- **Home Science** — B.Sc, M.Sc in Nutrition, Human Development, Textiles
- **Vidyapith (school level)** — Classes VI through XII

## Fees, hostel and residential campus

Banasthali is fully residential. Every admitted student is allotted a hostel room based on preference (bhawan / block). Fee schedules vary by programme and are published on the University website. Hostel and mess charges are included in the annual fee.
