# Frequently asked questions

## About the University

**Where is Banasthali Vidyapith located?**
Banasthali Vidyapith is in **Banasthali, Tonk district, Rajasthan**, roughly 75 km south of Jaipur on the Jaipur-Kota national highway. The nearest railway stations are Banasthali-Newai (5 km) and Jaipur (75 km); the nearest airport is Jaipur (Sanganer).

**Is Banasthali a government or private university?**
Banasthali is a **deemed-to-be university** under the Ministry of Education, Government of India (declared under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, in 1983). It is autonomous and self-financed, accredited by NAAC with A++ grade.

**Is Banasthali really women-only?**
Yes. All students are women. Male faculty and staff work in academic and administrative roles, but hostels, mess and student areas are women-only. The campus is entirely designed for women's residential education.

## Admissions

**When does the admission cycle open?**
The 2026-27 cycle opens for enquiries in October 2025 and for applications in January 2026. BUAT is typically held between May and July.

**Is BUAT compulsory?**
For most UG and PG programmes, yes. Certain PhD tracks, lateral entries and some diplomas may have programme-specific tests instead. Check the programme-specific eligibility on banasthali.org.

**Can I apply for more than one programme?**
Yes, you can indicate multiple programme preferences in a single application. The University may allocate the best-fit seat based on your BUAT rank and stated preferences.

**What is the age limit?**
Age limits are programme-specific. Vidyapith (school) has no upper age. UG programmes generally require the candidate to be at most 20-21 on 1 July of the admission year; PG has no upper age.

## Fees and finances

**What is the total fee?**
See the Fees knowledge article — annual fees range from ₹1.1 lakh (Vidyapith) to ₹3 lakh (MBA / B.Tech), all-inclusive of tuition, hostel and mess.

**Are there scholarships?**
Yes — merit, need-based, and government schemes (SC/ST/OBC/EWS). Apply through the student welfare office within the first 60 days.

**Can I pay in instalments?**
Fees are semester-wise (September and February). Within a semester, one-time payment is the norm; hardship cases may be handled individually — talk to the accounts office.

## Life on campus

**Do I have to stay in the hostel?**
Yes. Banasthali is fully residential. Every admitted student stays on campus except in exceptional cases approved by the Vice-Chancellor.

**Can I bring my phone / laptop?**
Yes. Personal phones and laptops are permitted. There is a fair-use Wi-Fi policy.

**Can I go home on weekends?**
Weekend outings need warden approval and parent confirmation. Home visits usually happen at semester breaks and long holidays.

**What if I need medical care?**
The campus health centre is open 24×7 with nurses and a visiting doctor. For anything serious, the University has tie-ups with hospitals in Jaipur and provides ambulance transport.
