# BUAT — Banasthali University Aptitude Test

The Banasthali University Aptitude Test (BUAT) is the University's own admission test. Most undergraduate and many postgraduate programmes require BUAT — the notable exceptions are certain PhD programmes, some school-to-college diploma routes, and lateral entry cases.

## Registration

Register for BUAT through the online admission portal on banasthali.org. Registration is included with your admission application; there is no separate BUAT-only sign-up. Registration typically opens in February and closes in July of the same admission cycle.

## Admit card

Admit cards are issued about 10–14 days before the test date, published in the applicant's portal account and sent to her registered WhatsApp / email. Print the admit card and carry it to the centre along with one government-issued photo ID.

## Exam pattern

BUAT is a written test lasting **2 hours** with **200 marks**. The pattern varies slightly by programme group:

- **B.Tech / BCA / M.Sc IT** — Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, General Aptitude
- **Life Sciences PG / M.Sc Biotech** — Biology, Chemistry, Reasoning, English
- **BA / MA / B.Ed** — English, Hindi, General Awareness, Reasoning, Programme-specific
- **Law** — Legal Reasoning, English, General Knowledge, Logical Reasoning
- **Home Science / Fine Arts** — Subject-specific portfolio review may replace part of the written test

## Merit list & normalisation

The merit list is programme-specific and published 7–10 days after the BUAT result. The final rank is computed from:

- **BUAT score** (weight varies by programme, typically 60–70%)
- **Normalised 10+2 aggregate** (typically 30–40%)

Normalisation adjusts for board-to-board variation (CBSE, state boards, ICSE, IB). Aap ka final rank aapke BUAT score aur 10+2 percentage dono ke aadhaar par decide hota hai.

## Result and next steps

- Merit list is published on the admissions portal and pushed to your WhatsApp + email.
- Called candidates receive a **counselling call letter** with a scheduled slot.
- Waitlisted candidates move up in subsequent rounds if seats free up.
- Rejected / not-called candidates can apply through the Second Round if seats remain.

## Test centres

BUAT is conducted at multiple centres across India — Delhi, Jaipur, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Chennai, Lucknow, Ahmedabad, Pune, and on-campus at Banasthali. Choose your centre during registration.
