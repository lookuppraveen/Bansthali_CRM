# Orientation & first days on campus

Once you have paid your admission fee and completed document verification, you are ready to join. Orientation typically happens in the **last week of August** each year (Orientation Week 2026 is scheduled from **28 August to 3 September**).

## Reporting on campus

- **Report to Banasthali campus** on the date printed on your admission letter.
- Enter through the **main gate** — security will check your admit letter and photo ID.
- Head to your assigned **bhawan** (allotment is on your portal at least 3 days before reporting).
- The warden will assign your room, hand over the key and get your basic paperwork signed.

## What to bring on arrival

- Original documents for final verification (10+2 marksheet, category certificate, transfer certificate, etc.)
- Admission letter (printed)
- Photo ID (Aadhaar / passport)
- Bedding, personal toiletries, initial clothing (2-3 days' worth is enough — the campus provisions store stocks basics)
- Basic medicines you take regularly, with prescription
- Laptop (recommended, not compulsory for all programmes)
- One small padlock for your cupboard

## Orientation programme

Orientation is compulsory. It runs over 5-7 days and covers:

- **Day 1** — Welcome by the Vice-Chancellor, campus tour, meet-your-faculty, ice-breakers
- **Day 2** — Bhawan induction, warden briefing, roommate introductions, campus rules
- **Day 3** — Panchmukhi induction — try each of the five dimensions (yoga, music, arts, community service, prayer meeting)
- **Day 4** — Academic orientation — meet your programme head, get your course outline, book library card
- **Day 5** — Health check-ups, ID card issuance, mess allocation
- **Weekend** — Bonding activities, prefect elections, first-Sunday cultural evening

## Classes begin

- Classes for the 2026-27 session begin on **2 September 2026**.
- The academic year runs September to April with two semesters.
- Winter break is approximately mid-December to first week of January.
- Summer break is May and June.

## First-week tips from senior students

- Attend everything in orientation week — you will make your closest friends here.
- Learn everyone in your bhawan wing by first name in the first week.
- Sign up for a music / dance / horse-riding class early — slots fill fast.
- Explore the campus — the library, the sports complex, the horse stables, the temple, the arts building.
- If you feel homesick, talk to your warden or a peer mentor. It's normal in the first two weeks.
- Set up UPI on your phone and add the campus provisions store as a favourite.

## Registration for orientation

Orientation registration is done through your student portal after your seat is confirmed. **Confirm your attendance by 26 August** so bhawan allocation, mess numbers and welcome kits are ready.
