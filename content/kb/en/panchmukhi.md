# Panchmukhi Shiksha — The Five-fold Education of Banasthali

Banasthali Vidyapith was founded by Pandit Hiralal Shastri and Smt. Ratan Shastri in **1935**. Its founding philosophy is **Panchmukhi Shiksha** — an integrated five-fold education that develops a woman across five dimensions, not just the intellectual.

## The five dimensions

1. **शारीरिक (Sharirik) — Physical**
   Yoga, sports, horse-riding, athletics, march-past, swimming. Every Banasthali student learns to ride a horse — a signature part of campus life. Daily physical activity is compulsory.

2. **व्यावहारिक (Vyavaharik) — Practical**
   Community service, crafts, cooking, first-aid, farm work, gardening. Every student contributes work-hours to the running of the campus. This teaches self-reliance and dignity of labour.

3. **कलात्मक (Kalatmak) — Aesthetic**
   Music (vocal, instrumental), dance (classical & folk), fine arts, painting, textiles, sculpture. Aesthetic sensibility is developed as a core part of every student's education, regardless of programme.

4. **नैतिक (Naitik) — Moral / Ethical**
   Daily prayer meetings (prarthana), values-based living, sarva-dharma-sambhava (respect for all faiths), moral instruction embedded in campus routine, service to community and nation.

5. **बौद्धिक (Bauddhik) — Intellectual**
   Rigorous academic programmes across arts, sciences, technology, law, management, education, life sciences, home science, music and fine arts. This is the dimension most universities focus on exclusively; at Banasthali it is one of five.

## Why this matters for admissions

If you are considering Banasthali, understand that you are choosing an education that shapes character and skills alongside academic degrees. The Panchmukhi routine is compulsory, not optional. Your daily schedule as a student will include:

- **Morning prarthana** (prayer meeting)
- **Physical activity** (sports / yoga / horse-riding)
- **Academic classes** (the standard programme curriculum)
- **Aesthetic activity** (music / arts of your choice)
- **Community / practical work** (rotational assignments)

Students who thrive here are those who embrace this holistic view. Students looking for a purely academic urban college experience may find Banasthali culturally different.

## The residential setting

The Panchmukhi ideal is inseparable from the fully residential nature of the campus. Living, learning, playing, praying and working together on a women's-only campus is part of the design — it is why Banasthali is distinctive among Indian universities.

## Founder's vision

Pandit Hiralal Shastri established Banasthali as a memorial to his daughter Shanta, with the vision of an educational institution that would empower Indian women to be **complete individuals** — physically strong, practically skilled, aesthetically sensitive, morally grounded, and intellectually rigorous.
